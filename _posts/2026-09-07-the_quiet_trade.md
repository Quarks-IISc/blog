We learn early that a mind is an inventory. <br>
You take the shelf space given to you,<br>
Stack the formulas where the sunlight won't fade them,<br>
And dust the facts that guarantee a future.<br><br>

Education here is a quiet trade.<br>
You exchange the noise of the street for the discipline of a ruled page.<br>
You swallow the sudden revisions in the middle of term,<br>
The clean tear where uncomfortable centuries used to sit,<br>
And learn to read off pages still damp from the press.<br><br>

It is about training the hand not to shake.<br>
Filling four thousand small bubbles without a stray smudge,<br>
Pretending that merit is a clean arithmetic <br>
Unbothered by who owns the printing press <br>
Or who buys the leak.<br>
