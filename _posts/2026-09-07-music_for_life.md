Music is not just a background hobby—it is the very rhythm of my daily existence. Accompanying me through 75% to 80% of their waking hours, music serves as a constant companion that bridges the gaps between study sessions, late-night reflections, routine walks, and moments of quiet boredom. Unbound by cultural barriers, my deep connection to sound spans across multiple languages, acting as a universal anchor that brings focus, comfort, and seamless harmony to every part of their day.
